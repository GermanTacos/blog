Now that the season has started properly, let's look at some of the predictions I made last month, and see what has changed.

It is worth noting that I am super amateur at basketball analytics and this is entirely for fun.

**New York Knicks**: The Knicks go 3-0 to start their season, and I couldn't be happier. I expect that they'll bounce back, but it's likely that another team will catch up before they work out their issues and start winning later on in the season.

**Detroit Pistons**: I was expecting that the loss of Isaiah Stewart was going to mean a shift away from the defensive focus the team kept last season. What I didn't expect was that this Pistons team would be shooting the lights out 24/7. I felt strange putting the 1 seed from the 2026 season at 8, but wasn't super convinced until I saw them play.

**Miami Heat**: This is exactly what I knew was going to go down. I moved the Heat up a little bit from my initial prediction (before my first post) because *everyone* was raving about how good they were going to be. But Miami has proven to be Milwaukee-with-palm-trees for Giannis.

**Toronto Raptors**: The team with Kawhi is impressing me more than I thought it would. The defense is staggering, and surprisingly, the team is winning more games than I thought they would. We'll see if the others can hold it down while Kawhi is out.

My team to keep an eye on this month: The **Toronto Raptors**.
