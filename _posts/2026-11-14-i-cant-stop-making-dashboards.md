Following up on my [recently-released](https://blog.tobographic.xyz/posts/i-made-an-nba-dashboard/) NBA dashboard, I have rounded out a complete set of dashboards for all of the basketball leagues that I follow.

[Visit my NBA Dashboard here](https://tobographic.xyz/utils/nba/)

The set is a total mash-up of different levels, types, and even different countries. The NBA dashboard remains unchanged, and the main league that I follow. I've covered the league on this blog before, but I haven't ever talked about the other three that I follow.

The second dashboard I made was for the Japanese B.LEAGUE PREMIER (spelled in all caps because the Japanese loves capital English letters), which is the top-level league in Japan. The data is pretty easy to parse, even if it required a custom proxy, and it includes as much translation to English as I can feasibly achieve at a glance: It tracks live games in the same manner that the NBA dashboard does, too. At some point I'd like to even include links to livestreams of the game, since the B.LEAGUE streams many of their games for free with English commentary.

[Visit my B.LEAGUE Dashboard here](https://tobographic.xyz/utils/b.league/)

After that one went up, I figured I had to do another for Unrivaled, the women's 3-on-3, full-court league that is just way too fun to not watch. That one is a very small association, with only eight clubs total: Live data is yet-untested since Unrivaled is only in season during the winter. Come January, I hope to work on it a bit more.

[Visit my Unrivaled Dashboard here](https://tobographic.xyz/utils/unrivaled/)

Once those were done, I figured I'd be remiss if I didn't do one for the MEC, in which my brother plays. That one is pretty simple too, since the
 conference is twelve teams, but it also tracks live and includes (vague) information about any college team that they play when a game is live 
and being tracked (scrimmages probably don't have that kind of data). It's unlikely that I'd need to see that data *myself*, given that I attend many of those games, but its nice to show off to friends and family. Maybe someone will find some utility in it?

[Visit my Mountain East Conference Dashboard here](https://tobographic.xyz/utils/mec/)

It just goes to show that I can't be left alone with templates. I want to make everything humanly possible out of them.
